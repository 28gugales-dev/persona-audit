The ancients sought and found what was to them a satisfactory etymology. Istros, writing in the third century B.c. and quoted by Harpocration, says that Anthesterion is the blossoming month because then ‘the most of the things that spring from the earth blossom forth’. The ELtymologicon Magnum offers an easy-going alternative: feast and month bear their names either because the earth then began to blossom, or because they offered flowers at the festival.
