But the season at which these fire -festivals are most generally held all over Europe is the summer solstice, that is, Midsummer Eve (23d June) or Midsummer Day (24th June). According to a mediEeval writer the three great features of this festival were the bonfires, the procession with torches round the fields, and the custom of rolling a wheel.
