Observe, then, that leaves are broadly divisible into mainsails and studding-sails; but that the word _leaf_ is properly to be used only of the mainsail; leaflet is the best word for minor divisions; and whether these minor members are only separated by deep cuts, or become complete stalked leaflets, still they are always to be thought of merely as parts of a true leaf.
