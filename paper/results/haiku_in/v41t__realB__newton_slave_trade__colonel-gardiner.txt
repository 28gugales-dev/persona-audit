Nor are we without remarkable displays of the same sovereign, efficacious grace in our own times ; — I may particularly mention the instance of the late colonel Gardiner. If any real satisfaction could be found in a sinful course, he would have met with it ; for he pursued the experiment with all possible advantages. He was habituated to evil ; and many uncommon, almost miraculous deliverances, made no impression upon him. Yet he was likewise made willing in the day of God’s power : and the bright example of his life, illustrated and diffused by the account of him, published since his death, has afforded an occasion of much praise to God, and much comfort to his people.
