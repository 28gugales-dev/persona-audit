- questions, doubt, or being less sure than before

Having now much leisure, I prosecuted the study of the Latin with good success. I remembered a dictionary this voyage, and procured two or three other books; but still it was my hap to choose the hardest. I added Juvenal to Horace ; and, for prose authors, I nitched upon Livy, Caesar, and Sallust. You will easily conceive, Sir, that I had hard work to begin (where I should have left off ) with Horace and Livy. I was not aware of the difference of style ; I had heard Livy highly commended, and was resolved to understand him. I began with the first page, and laid down a rule, which I seldom departed from, not to proceed to a second period till I understood the first, and so on.
