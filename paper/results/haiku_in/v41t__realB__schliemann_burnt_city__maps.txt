I cannot refrain from making on this occasion a warm acknowledgment to both Admiral Spratt and Commander Graves for the immense service they have rendered to science by their most excellent map of the Troad. Nothing has escaped the close scrutiny they gave to every spot, in order to produce as complete a map of the plain and the hills falling into it as was possible, as a basis for the future study of Homeric Topography. For all previous maps were mere compilations of many tyravellers’ journeys, and so in many points very erroneous and confusing, as well as deficient in giving the necessary geographical details.
